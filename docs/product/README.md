# Product

**Challenge:** Beyond the List (PostFinance). Turn the transaction list into insight.

**Who for:** migrants, refugees and people on low incomes in Switzerland.

**What they get:** a monthly report in plain words, a dashboard they reshape by asking, savings tips with cheaper alternatives, alerts on suspicious payments, and PostFinance investment ideas.

**Out of scope for the MVP:** live bank connection; receipt upload (low priority).
